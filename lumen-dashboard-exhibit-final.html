SUPPORTING EVIDENCE · MARKET, COST, COMPETITOR & RISK DETAIL

1. Market Justification: Why Germany?

  • Scale: the case estimates a €9.1bn German functional-beverage market in 2026, roughly 35% of European category value, with approximately 7% CAGR through 2033.
  • Demand readiness: four established competitors already validate the category, while LUMEN brings three years of repeat-purchase evidence from the Netherlands, Denmark and Sweden and roughly €3.2m trailing revenue across those markets.
  • Economic capacity: Germany had 83.6m people in 2025 and a GDP of approximately €4.47tn; purchasing power is above the EU average, supporting a premium-accessible €2.19 price point. Destatis

2. Historical Context & Market Evolution

  • 2022–24: LUMEN launched in Copenhagen and expanded across NL/DK/SE; the category matured from niche energy products toward low-sugar, natural-caffeine and functional-positioning propositions.
  • Current conditions: German GDP contracted 0.5% in 2024, so consumers and retailers are likely to scrutinise price and repeat value more closely than brand story alone. Destatis
  • Structural shifts: omnichannel discovery, retailer concentration, deposit/packaging obligations and stricter sustainability expectations raise the value of a controlled DTC test before broad retail rollout. Packaging obligations are governed by Germany’s packaging framework. VerpackDG
